How to Scan Your Hair

1

Open the Scanner

Tap the scan icon (+) in the bottom navigation bar. Accept the camera permission when prompted and review the disclaimer.

2

Position & Capture

Hold your phone 6–8 inches from your scalp in good lighting. Align the target area within the frame and tap the capture button.

3

Review Your Results

The app will analyze follicle density, coverage, and scalp health. View your personalized results and recommended protocol on the results screen.
