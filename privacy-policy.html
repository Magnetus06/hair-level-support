6. AI-Powered Analysis & Transparency

In accordance with the EU AI Act and Apple's guidelines on AI transparency:

  • What the AI does: When you take a scan, your hair/scalp image is sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o Vision API, which analyzes follicle density, coverage patterns, and overall scalp health.
  • How it works: The AI returns a structured assessment including a Norwood scale estimation, density score, and personalized observations. These results are informational only and are not a medical diagnosis.
  • Data handling by OpenAI: Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, images submitted via their API are not used to train their models. Images are retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted.
  • Your control: You decide when to initiate a scan. No images are captured or transmitted without your active participation. You can delete all stored scan results at any time from within the App.
  • Limitations: AI analysis accuracy depends on image quality, lighting, and angle. Results may vary between scans and should not replace professional medical evaluation.
EU AI Act Classification: This application is classified as a limited-risk AI system. We provide this transparency notice to ensure you are informed that AI is being used to process your images.

7. Biometric & Sensitive Data

Hair and scalp images may be considered health-related data under GDPR Article 9. We handle this data with the following safeguards:

  • Processing is based on your explicit consent, obtained before each scan.
  • Images are transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS 1.2+).
  • Analysis results and images are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • You may withdraw consent and delete all data at any time.

10. Your Rights

Under GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15) — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16) — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17) — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18) — Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21) — Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)) — Withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint — File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

Under CCPA/CPRA (California Users)

  • Right to Know — What personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Right to Delete — Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale — We do not sell your personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hairlevel.support@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days (or within the legally required timeframe in your jurisdiction).

13. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Minimal data collection: We follow the principle of data minimization — collecting only what is necessary to provide the service.

While no system is 100% secure, we continuously review and improve our security practices.
