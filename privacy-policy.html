2. Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

Onboarding Data Age range, hair type, hair concerns, gender Personalized protocol generation
Usage Data Task completions, scan history, streak data App functionality, progress tracking

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or browsing history. The App does not require account creation.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Hair Analysis: Your scalp/hair images are sent over encrypted endpoints to OpenAI's API for temporary, real-time AI-powered density analysis. No biometric Face Data, facial mapping coordinates, or distinct structural facial meshes are read, extracted, or processed at any time. Calculated scores are returned to your device, displayed in-app, and stored locally.
  • Protocol Generation: Onboarding answers are used to generate a personalized hair care protocol tailored to your needs.
  • Progress Tracking: Scan results and task completions are stored locally to show your progress over
